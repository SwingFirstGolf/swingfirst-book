Operator Tee Sheet Guide

For SwingFirst golf operations staff. Last updated August 2026.

Overview

The SwingFirst operator tee sheet is your real-time view of every tee time across all SwingFirst courses. It syncs automatically with Quick18 every few minutes, pulling bookings, player names, rates, and check-in status.

Because the sheet reflects Quick18 as the source of truth, changes made directly in Quick18 will appear here within one sync cycle. Changes made here — check-ins, blocks, consolidations — are stored in SwingFirst's own database and displayed on top of the synced data.

Quick18 vs. SwingFirst

Booking creation and cancellation still happens in Quick18. The operator sheet is for day-of management: moving players, checking in, blocking times, and reading member data.

Navigating the Sheet

Use the course selector in the top bar to switch between courses. The date picker lets you view today or tomorrow. The sheet always loads to today by default.

Each row is a tee time. The left column shows the time and rack rate for that slot. The four columns to the right are the four player positions.

Player Cells

Each booked cell shows the player's name, any membership badges, and the price they paid. The background color indicates group size.

Group color coding

Players who are part of the same Q18 reservation share a background color. A solo booking is white. A twosome is light yellow. Three or four players in one reservation shift to a slightly deeper shade. This lets you instantly see which players belong together when multiple bookings share a tee time.

Corner triangle

A small colored triangle in the bottom-right corner of a cell reflects the color that Quick18 assigned to that reservation. It matches what you'd see on the Quick18 tee sheet and has no operational meaning beyond visual grouping.

Consolidation arrows

An up (↑) or down (↓) arrow badge means this player was moved to this tee time from an earlier or later slot during a consolidation. It's a reminder that the original booking was at a different time.

Membership Badges

Badges appear directly below a player's name in their cell. They are pulled from the membership code Quick18 embeds in the booking, as well as from the player's full booking history. A player may show more than one badge if they hold multiple card types.

Multiple badges

If a player shows more than one badge, the first badge (leftmost) reflects the rate code used for this specific booking. Additional badges are from their booking history — they hold those cards too, but didn't book under them today.

DIA

DIA (Days In Advance) is how many days before the tee time the booking was made. For SwingFirst cardholders, a DIA of 7 or more is required for the SF rate to be valid.

Adding Players

Click the + in any open player position to open the Add Player modal. Fill in the player's name, rate, and price, then save. The new player will appear in that position immediately.

The name field has autocomplete — start typing and matching names from past bookings will appear, along with their membership badges. Selecting a name fills in the name field; it does not auto-fill the rate (staff should confirm the correct rate applies for that day).

Moving & Copying Players

Drag and drop

Drag a player cell to any other tee time to move them. If the player is in position 1 (lead), dragging will prompt you to choose whether to move just that player or the entire booking group.

Moving multiple players

Hold Ctrl and click to select multiple players within the same tee time, then drag the group to a new slot.

Copying instead of moving

Hold Cmd while dragging to copy a player to the destination rather than moving them. The original remains in place.

Cut / Copy / Paste via right-click

Right-click any player cell to access the context menu. You can cut, copy, or paste players. After copying, right-click any open slot and choose Paste to place the player there.

Moving a group

When players share a booking (same Q18 reservation color), moving the lead player will offer to move the whole group together. Use this when a party needs to be bumped to a different tee time.

Checking In

Right-click a player and select Check In. Their cell will update to reflect checked-in status and the tooltip will show "Checked In ✓".

To undo a check-in, right-click and select Undo Check-In.

Check-in status is stored in SwingFirst and does not write back to Quick18.

Editing a Booking

Right-click a player and choose Edit to open the edit booking screen. From there you can update the player's name, rate type, price, and notes.

The name field in the edit screen has the same autocomplete as the add screen — type to search past players by name. Results show "Last, First" and display their membership badges.

Cancelling a Player

Right-click a player and choose Cancel. You'll be asked to confirm. The player is removed from that position and the slot becomes open again.

Cancellations in the operator sheet are local to SwingFirst. If the booking also needs to be cancelled in Quick18, that must be done separately in Quick18.

Blocking Tee Times

Blocking a single tee time

Right-click any empty tee time row and choose Block. A modal will appear where you can add a label (e.g. "Aeration", "Tournament Hold"). Save to apply the block.

Bulk blocking

To block a range of tee times at once, hold Ctrl and drag across the time column to select multiple slots. A selection bar will appear at the bottom of the screen showing how many slots are selected. Click Block Selected to block them all in one step with a shared label.

Unblocking

Right-click a blocked slot and choose Unblock.

Blocked slots and sync

If a tee time is blocked in Quick18, it will appear as blocked here automatically on the next sync. Blocks created in the operator sheet are independent of Quick18.

Rack Rate & Pricing

Current rack rate

The rack rate shown in the left column of each row is the current price for that tee time as of the last sync. It may change between syncs if you've updated pricing in Quick18.

Locked rate

When a tee time is booked, the rack rate at that moment is locked to that booking and never changes. This is the rate that governs all discounts for that booking, regardless of what the rack rate is today.

You can see the locked rate and the current rack rate side by side in the player tooltip when they differ.

SwingFirst cardholder rate

For any player with an SF badge, the tooltip shows an SF rate — calculated as 15% off the locked rate. This is the correct rate to charge that player. The discount applies individually: only players in the group who are SF cardholders receive it.

Example

A tee time was booked at $45. The rack rate today is $38 due to dynamic pricing. An SF cardholder in that group should be charged $45 × 0.85 = $38.25 — based on the locked rate, not today's rack.

Consolidation

Consolidation is the process of moving sparse bookings earlier or later in the day to free up blocks of consecutive open tee times. It's typically done in the morning before the course opens.

When a player has been moved via consolidation, a small arrow badge appears on their cell — ↑ for moved earlier, ↓ for moved later. Hovering the tooltip will show "Moved earlier" or "Moved later" in the Note row.

Consolidation is done manually using drag-and-drop. There is no automatic consolidation.

Customer Search

Click Customers in the left navigation to open the customer drawer. Type at least two characters to search by name.

Search results show each matching player's booking history, including the membership types they've used across all courses and dates. This is useful for verifying a player's membership status or looking up how many times they've played.

The customer drawer does not create or edit bookings — it's a read-only lookup tool.

Membership verification

If a player at the window claims to have an SF card but it doesn't show on today's booking, search for them in the Customer drawer. Their historical membership types will appear, which can help confirm whether they've held that card before.

Sync & Data Freshness

The sheet syncs with Quick18 automatically every few minutes. The sync pulls today's and tomorrow's tee sheet for every course simultaneously.

To force an immediate refresh, use the Reload button in the top bar.

Pausing sync for a facility

Each facility can have its sync paused independently. This is useful during system maintenance or if you need to prevent Quick18 data from overwriting local changes temporarily. The pause toggle is available in the settings area. Paused facilities are noted in the sync log.
